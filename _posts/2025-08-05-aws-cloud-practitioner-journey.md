After earning my **AWS AI Practitioner** certification on **June 6, 2025**, I was eager to keep my AWS momentum going. To deepen my understanding of core AWS services and gain a broad overview of the ecosystem, I decided to tackle the **AWS Cloud Practitioner** exam next. Two weeks later, I sat for the exam — and I'm excited to say I passed with a score of **807/1000**! ✅ You can check my [verification link here](https://cp.certmetrics.com/amazon/en/public/verify/credential/d330225c890e4f759169e885dce08d2f).

## My Exam Preparation

This was my second AWS certification. After earning the AI Practitioner, I wanted to strengthen my foundational understanding of the AWS ecosystem before jumping into the SAA-C03. Here's what my preparation looked like.

### 1. AWS Skill Builder

AWS provides excellent foundational courses on AWS Skill Builder. The main ones I completed:

- **Cloud Practitioner Essentials** — a broad overview of AWS Cloud covering core concepts, security, architecture, pricing, and support.
- **Getting Started with AWS Cloud Essentials** — a beginner-friendly course exploring cloud computing, core services, and the basics of networking, security, and architecture.
- **AWS Technical Essentials** — a practical introduction with hands-on labs for EC2, S3, RDS, VPC, IAM, and more.
- **Exam Prep Standard Course: AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02)** — directly aligned to the exam objectives, very helpful for matching my knowledge to what would actually be tested.

### 2. Udemy — Stéphane Maarek's CLF-C02 Course

I followed Stéphane Maarek's highly regarded **Ultimate AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner CLF-C02** course and explored every part thoroughly:

- Practiced hands-on labs using my own AWS Free Tier account.
- Reviewed the 500+ pages of PDF notes multiple times before the exam.
- Learned from real-world examples that made the concepts much easier to remember and apply.

### 3. Practice Exams

I practiced extensively with **Udemy's 6 Practice Exams | AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner CLF-C02** (390 questions), reviewing every answer thoroughly to understand the logic behind it.

### 4. Extra Resources

While preparing, I found the [AWS-Solutions-Architect-Associate-Exam-2025 repo by sv222](https://github.com/sv222/AWS-Solutions-Architect-Associate-Exam-2025). Although built for the SAA-C03 exam, it offers a well-organized overview of the most-used AWS services and architectures — very helpful for polishing my knowledge and seeing the bigger picture.

## What I Learned

While my final score wasn't as high as I'd hoped (I was aiming for 900+), I'm proud of the progress. The hands-on labs, practice questions, and review materials built a solid foundation that will help as I aim for the **AWS Solutions Architect — Associate (SAA-C03)** next.
